TITLE: Japanese: the "Writing" status of a running reply is translated as 書き込み中
LABELS: bug,severity: low
## What happens
While a reply streams, the status line says 書き込み中 in Japanese. 書き込み中 reads as "writing to storage", not "the model is writing a reply".

## Steps
1. Set the interface language to Japanese.
2. Send a message and look at the status line while the reply streams.

develop 3ada3c9, Japanese.
