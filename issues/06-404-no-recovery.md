## What happens
When the provider answers that the selected model does not exist (404), the reply shows "The provider does not offer this model" with only a Details button. Other provider failures offer Retry or Sign in; this one offers nothing, and nothing points to the model picker.

## Steps
1. Select a model, then make the provider stop offering it.
2. Send a message.

## Environment
develop 3ada3c9, desktop browser and all tested devices, English and Japanese, fixture provider returning 404.
