TITLE: Touch devices: session row actions (Pin session, Session actions) are invisible until hover
LABELS: bug,severity: medium
## What happens
On iPhone and iPad, the Pin session and Session actions buttons on every session row, and a folder's Remove button, stay at opacity 0 because they only appear on hover. Touch has no hover, so a person cannot see that the actions exist. Their touch targets are 24 x 24 px.

## Steps
1. Open the web client in Safari on an iPhone or iPad.
2. Open the Workspaces drawer and look at a session row.

## Environment
develop 3ada3c9, Safari on iOS 26.5 (iOS Simulator, iPhone 17 and iPad Air 11"), real taps.
