TITLE: Mobile Safari: the Workspaces drawer is taller than the screen and its Settings button is below the bottom edge
LABELS: bug,severity: medium
## What happens
In Safari on iPhone and iPad, in portrait and landscape, the Workspaces drawer is 29-40 px taller than the visible screen. The Settings button at its bottom is below the bottom edge and cannot be tapped.

| Device | Screen height | Settings button centre |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone 17 portrait | 714 px | y 726 |
| iPad Air 11" portrait | 1094 px | y 1095 |
| iPad Air 11" landscape | 734 px | y 735 |

Desktop browsers do not show this.

## Steps
1. Open the web client in Safari on an iPhone or iPad.
2. Open the Workspaces drawer and look for Settings at its bottom.

## Environment
develop 3ada3c9, Safari on iOS 26.5 (iOS Simulator), real taps, English and Japanese.
