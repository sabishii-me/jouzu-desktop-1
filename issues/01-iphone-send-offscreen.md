TITLE: iPhone Safari: after the first message the conversation and composer are wider than the screen; Send is off screen and text is cut off
LABELS: bug,severity: high
## What happens
In Safari on iPhone, once a session has a message, the conversation column and the composer are 520 px wide on a 402 px wide screen. The page cannot scroll sideways (document scroll width is 402 px).
- The Send button sits at x 423-497, entirely off screen, so a second message cannot be sent.
- Every paragraph of a long message runs 74-101 px past the right edge: plain text, a long URL, Japanese text and a long identifier are all cut off, in the user's message and in the reply.

On the empty new-session screen Send is still on screen (x 299-373). On iPad the text wraps within the screen and Send stays tappable.

## Steps
1. Open the web client in Safari on an iPhone.
2. Close the Workspaces drawer, start a new session and send a long message (a long sentence, a long URL, Japanese text).
3. Wait for the reply and try to send a second message.

## Environment
develop 3ada3c9, Safari on iOS 26.5 (iOS Simulator, iPhone 17 portrait, 402 x 714), real taps, English and Japanese, fixture model.
